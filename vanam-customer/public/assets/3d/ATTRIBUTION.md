# 3D Asset Attribution — VANAM storefront

All 3D geometry and textures currently used by the storefront are **original**:
procedural `three` geometry and `<canvas>`-generated wood-grain textures
(`src/three/`). No third-party models are bundled.

When a CC-BY (or similarly attribution-required) asset is added, credit it here
with: asset name, author, source URL, and license. See `docs/ASSET_LICENSES.md`
for the full policy and the master record.
